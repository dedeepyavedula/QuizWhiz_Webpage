QuizWhiz

QuizWhiz is a dynamic, entry-level platform designed specifically for aspiring developers to test and solidify their foundational coding knowledge. Tailored for beginners, the site offers a curated selection of interactive quizzes covering the "Big Three" of software development: C++, Python, and Java alongside essential Web Development modules like HTML and CSS. Each quiz is structured to reinforce core concepts such as syntax, loops, and data types in a low-pressure environment, making it an ideal pitstop for students looking to "debug" their understanding before moving on to complex projects. With its clean, high-tech aesthetic and focus on clarity, QuizWhiz transforms the often-intimidating first steps of a computer science journey into an engaging, gamified experience.
